# Bibliography (working)

- James & Sabry -- *Theseus: A High Level Language for Reversible
  Computing* (the ancestor; clause syntax, labels).
- The Π papers (Sabry et al.) -- the core calculus and its extensions
  (fractional/negative types; "How to Bake a Quantum Π").
- Kaarsgaard et al. -- join inverse categories; semantics of partiality
  and iteration for reversible languages (ADR-0005).
- Paolini, Piccolo, Roversi -- *Reversible Primitive Permutations*
  (total reversible language; PR-completeness floor for v0.2, ADR-0007).
- Yokoyama & Glück -- Janus (imperative reversible baseline; `fi`
  assertions as the come-from discipline).
- Vieri / Frank et al. (MIT) -- Pendulum/PISA reversible ISA and silicon.
- Frank -- adiabatic logic families (2LAL, SCRL); the L4 syllabus.
- Maranget -- *Warnings for pattern matching* (the matrix algorithm
  behind ADR-0004).
- Mac Lane; Laplaza -- coherence (TH-3).
- Bennett 1973 -- reversible simulation via history tapes (why native
  reversibility means no garbage, ADR framing).
